# Governance

This document describes how projects in the `noru-tech` organization are governed.

## Overview

Noru's open-source repositories are maintained by Noru with input from the community. As a
compliance company, we hold our own projects to a high standard of traceability,
accountability, and security.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** — Noru team members responsible for the direction, review, and release
  of a repository. They triage issues, review pull requests, and have merge rights.
- **Contributors** — Anyone who submits issues, pull requests, or documentation. Everyone
  is welcome to contribute under our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and
  [Contributing guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Decision Making

- Most decisions are made through discussion on issues and pull requests, seeking
  consensus among maintainers.
- For significant or breaking changes, a maintainer will summarize the proposal and the
  rationale before a decision is finalized.
- Maintainers have the final say to keep projects coherent and secure. Where a change
  touches compliance logic, correctness and auditability take precedence over speed.

## Changes to Governance

Changes to this document are made by Noru maintainers via pull request so the history of
decisions remains transparent and reviewable.

## Contact

For questions about governance or maintainership, open a discussion or issue in the
relevant repository. For security matters, follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
