# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Noru community
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or
experience level. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of inappropriate or unwelcome language or imagery.
- Insulting, derogatory, or demeaning comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Our Standards Are Based On the Contributor Covenant

This Code of Conduct adopts the [Contributor Covenant][homepage], version 2.1, available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html. Please refer to it
for the full text, including detailed enforcement guidelines.

[homepage]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org

## Enforcement

complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders are

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — including all repositories in
the `noru-tech` organization — and also applies when an individual is officially
representing the community in public spaces.
